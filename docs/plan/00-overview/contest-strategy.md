# Contest strategy

## Entry
- Category: **Frankenstein** (Best Frankenstein $5k; also eligible for 1st-3rd overall).
- Bonus prizes to enter: Best Startup ($10k, needs founder confirmation, LinkedIn, startup website),
  Best Demo (automatic), Bonus Blog, Social Blitz.
- A project can win one overall or one category prize, plus any number of bonus prizes.

## Judging: five criteria, 20% each

| Criterion | How we win it | Proof in the submission |
|---|---|---|
| Potential impact | A real group uses it before Dec 4. Show a real (consenting) friend group's page. | Video clip + numbers in README (members, cards, days active) |
| Technological implementation | Deep, varied Kiro use: specs per phase, steering, hooks, MCP, property-based tests, a custom agent. | `.kiro/` folder, `04-build/kiro-usage.md` turned into the write-up |
| Design | One coherent haunted-scrapbook look, mobile-first, polished motion and sound. | Video, live app |
| Innovation | The stitching: dead web tech each doing a live job. The pet as a group-health signal and the spirit board are the novel parts. | First 30 s of video |
| Presentation | A tight story: problem, the page, the old-web parts, the AI that waits its turn, built with Kiro. | `05-submission/demo-script.md` |

## What 2025's winners had in common (from their Devpost pages)
- A clear real problem (Faxi: seniors offline; RumorNet: misinformation).
- Heavy, documented Kiro use (UnitSet: 21 specs, 15 steering docs, 9 hooks, 4 MCP servers; Faxi: 16+ specs,
  4 hooks, custom MCP servers).
- A strong Frankenstein architecture story (UnitSet, RumorNet).
- Not necessarily spooky, outside Costume Contest.

## Our weak spots and fixes
| Weak spot | Fix |
|---|---|
| Disclosed fork lowers Innovation | Lead with new features; show the gate and the pet, which judges won't have seen |
| 8 days | Strict cut order in `04-build/phases.md` |
| Impact feels like a toy | Real group usage, the receipt split, and the "group is drifting" pet signal |
| Trademarks in video | Generic names only (see `02-design/brand.md`) |

## Rough odds (judgment, not data)
Best Frankenstein 10-15%. Top 3 overall 3-5%. Any cash prize ~20% if the demo and Kiro write-up are strong.
Blog bonus is likely if the post ships.
