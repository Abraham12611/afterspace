# Vision

## The pitch (15 seconds)
Remember spending a whole night on your MySpace page? Your Top 8, your song, your glitter? It's all gone,
and your friends now live in a wall of group chats. Afterspace brings the page back, but for your group:
one live page where your plans, money, memories and inside jokes stay put, with a little ghost pet
that only stays alive if you actually show up for each other.

## Who it's for
- Primary: friend groups of 3-12 who already share a group chat (house-mates, long-distance friends,
  trip crews, a fantasy league).
- Secondary (startup angle): communities that want a "home page" rather than a feed: clubs, dorm floors,
  small Discord groups.

## The problem
1. Decisions, money owed and plans scroll away in chat.
2. Social networks became feeds of ads and strangers.
3. Nothing nudges a drifting group back together.

## What Afterspace does
- A live shared page per group: cards for polls, RSVPs, splits, countdowns, lists, photos, notes.
- Everything updates on every screen at once, with named cursors.
- Say or type what you need ("poll for saturday dinner") and the card appears. The AI never changes a card
  someone is holding.
- The old web, stitched back in: Top 8, profile song, buddy list and away messages, guestbook,
  visitor counter, a pet that lives off group activity, a spirit board for group decisions,
  sealed letters, and a séance that catches you up.

## Why it's "Frankenstein"
Each dead piece of the old web is stitched to a live modern system so it does a real job:

| Dead part | Live part | Job it does |
|---|---|---|
| MySpace Top 8 | Interaction graph from votes, claims, replies | Shows who you actually do things with |
| Profile song | y2k player card | Shared mood, tap-to-play |
| Buddy list, away messages, door creak | Convex presence | Who's here, who's away, without asking |
| Virtual pet | Activity-driven health, scheduler | A group-health signal you can feel |
| Spirit board | Multi-cursor input | A group decision nobody can rig alone |
| Sealed letters / time capsule | Scheduler | Notes that open on a date |
| Paper receipts | Vision model on OpenRouter | Photo of a receipt becomes a fair split |
| Séance | LLM digest | "Summon what you missed" |

## Success for the contest
A judge can open the live app with no signup, see two tabs sync, ask for a card, watch the gate hold an AI
change, feed the pet, and decide something on the spirit board, all within 3 minutes of video.
