# User flows (each must work end to end for the demo)

## U1. First visit, no signup
1. Open an invite link `#/join/coven`.
2. The page blurs behind "step inside": type a name, pick a look.
3. Step inside: door creak for others, your row turns green in the buddy list, your cursor appears for them.
**Check:** under 20 s from link to cursor, on a phone.

## U2. Two screens, one page
1. Phone and laptop on the same space.
2. Vote on the dinner poll on the phone; the bar moves on the laptop.
3. Drag a card on the laptop; it moves on the phone while dragging.
**Check:** no refresh anywhere.

## U3. Ask for a card
1. Tap the crystal ball, say "poll for friday game night".
2. A skeleton appears, then fills, then lands on every screen.
**Check:** card is valid with OpenRouter on; a canned version lands with no key.

## U4. The AI waits its turn
1. Phone: start typing in the dinner poll (hold).
2. Laptop: ask "add ramen to the dinner poll".
3. Laptop sees a ghost "waiting on wren"; when the phone lets go, ramen lands.
4. Laptop taps undo.
**Check:** the held card never changes while held.

## U5. The pet
1. The seeded space starts "hungry": the pet droops and says "nobody's voted on dinner yet".
2. Two people vote and RSVP; the pet perks up to "okay".
3. Admin fast-forward shows a second space whose pet died; its graveyard entry has an epitaph.
4. Three people act in that space; the pet revives as "wisp ii".

## U6. Spirit board
1. Add a spirit board: "where for the cabin trip?" with 4 answers.
2. Two tabs grab the planchette; it drifts to the average of both pointers.
3. It rests on "tahoe" for 3 s and locks. A single tab alone can't lock it.

## U7. Receipt split
1. On the split card, "scan a receipt", pick the sample receipt.
2. It reads "$84.00, Pizza Palace". Payer picks who's in (4), confirms.
3. "$21.00 each" shows to everyone.

## U8. Sealed letter
1. From the buddy list, write to Theo: "open tomorrow" (demo: 1 minute).
2. Theo sees a locked envelope; it unlocks; Theo opens it; you see "read".

## U9. Séance
1. Admin marks a member away for 2 days.
2. On return: "summon what you missed" shows 4-6 lines, each opens its card.

## U10. Profile header
1. Top 8 reflects who you did things with this week; tap a face to see why.
2. Visitor counter ticks up for a new member.
3. Profile song plays on tap.
