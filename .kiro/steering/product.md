# Product: Afterspace

## One line
MySpace died. Your friends' space shouldn't have. Afterspace is a live, shared page for a friend group,
stitched from the bones of the old web: Top 8, a profile song, a buddy list with away messages, a pet that
lives on your page, a spirit board for decisions, and letters that arrive sealed.

## Who it's for
Small friend groups (3-12 people) whose shared life is buried in group chats: house-mates, long-distance
friends, a fantasy league, a trip crew.

## Contest framing
Kiroween 2026, category **Frankenstein**. Bonus: Best Startup (if the founder paperwork is ready), Best Demo,
Blog, Social Blitz. Full plan: `docs/plan/`.

## Product rules
- No signup to join a space. A name and a look is enough (anonymous guest session).
- Everything is live. Nothing refreshes.
- The AI proposes, code decides. The AI never edits a card a person is holding (Right of Way).
- Lowercase, warm, human copy. Spooky is the costume, not the product: every haunted touch must do a job.
- Phones first.
- Never use third-party trademarks as feature names in UI or the video: say "spirit board", not Ouija;
  "pet", not Tamagotchi; "buddy list", not AIM; "y2k player", not Winamp. The write-up may name them as
  inspirations.
